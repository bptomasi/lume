  ── VIM READER MOTIONS ──────────────────────────────────────────────

  zt / zz / zb    Put cursor row at top / center / bottom

  zH / zL         Scroll code by half the reader width
  5j / 3W / 2e    Counts apply to motions; Esc cancels a prefix/count
  /               Find literal text inside the article

  :toc            Searchable heading outline; Enter jumps

  ── RECOMMENDATIONS ────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

  Space R / :recommendations    Open For you; runs locally without a model
  :recommend-why                Explain interests, source and observed evidence

  :recommend-feedback useful    Approve this article and its matching interests
  :recommend-feedback not-interested   Hide it; reduce matching affinities
  :recommend-feedback too-basic / too-advanced   Hide it without rejecting topic
  :recommend-feedback clear     Remove the rating; eligible articles can return

  Example: :interest Kernel networking | 9 | conntrack OR cilium OR XDP
  Terms support prefixes, phrases, AND/OR/NOT and parentheses. Explicit fields:
  title, body, source, author, domain and tag. Private notes never affect matches.
  Profile priorities dominate bounded topic feedback. Opening/reading is not
  approval. Favorites add a small bonus. A code block and announcement wording
  are observed signals, not proof of depth, correctness or editorial quality.

  For you contains at most 100 diverse entries from 512 scored candidates.
  Duplicate canonical URLs share one entry. Negative ratings hide duplicates.
  No age cutoff discards older reference material. New/changed text is indexed
  in durable batches of 64; read/favorite edits reuse text evidence. Work runs
  on a bounded background queue, and cached lists remain usable offline.
  Initial classification can take time for large libraries. Navigation never
  runs recommendation analysis. :recommend-why identifies feed/summary/full
  evidence; the recommender never downloads articles or sends personal data.
