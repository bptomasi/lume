# Full article extraction dependency notices

This bundle covers the 33 dependency additions for full article extraction,
including the published `tendril` decoder dependency restored in the lockfile.
Lume's own code remains MIT. Existing dependencies retain their own licenses.

This extraction integration includes the following MPL-2.0 transitives.
MPL-covered packages are `selectors`, `cssparser`, `cssparser-macros` and
`dtoa-short`. Their original source headers remain in the unmodified upstream
sources. Ship this notice bundle with redistributed binaries, retain source
notices, and make covered source available under MPL-2.0. The source links below
identify the exact published package versions; distributors must keep them
available or provide their own corresponding source distribution.

This inventory and retained texts do not constitute a complete product-wide
license or advisory audit. See `docs/dependency-security.md` for release policy.
